# Outreach style rules

Every rule has an ID. The verifier must cite rule IDs in every rejection —
"sounds off" is not a verdict here.

- **R1 — Length.** Body is 130 words or fewer. Busy people skim; long emails get archived.
- **R2 — No hype vocabulary.** None of: revolutionize, game-changing, cutting-edge,
  synergy, unlock, supercharge, disrupt, next-level, world-class. If the product is
  good, plain words carry it.
- **R3 — At most one exclamation mark** in the entire email, including the subject.
- **R4 — Concrete next step.** The email ends with exactly one specific, low-effort
  ask (a question or a proposed action with a timeframe). Not "let me know your
  thoughts", not two asks.
- **R5 — Subject line.** Present, first line, `Subject: ` prefix, 60 characters or
  fewer after the prefix, no clickbait ("quick question", "re:" on a first touch).
- **R6 — No unresolved placeholders.** Nothing like `[Name]`, `{{company}}`, or
  `<insert X>` may survive into the final draft.
- **R7 — One claim, one basis.** Any factual claim about the recipient's company must
  come from the brief. The worker may not invent funding rounds, launches, or metrics.
- **R8 — Sign-off.** Ends with the sender name given in the brief, no title inflation.
